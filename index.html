How to read this

Cost, clicks, impressions, leads, sales and revenue are credited by last click to the Fall Summit 2026 Meta ad sets. No lead tag is applied, so sales include anyone Hyros credits to these ad sets. Hyros cost can trail Meta's own spend by a few hours, and Hyros caches a repeated query for about four minutes. Revenue is last-click, so a sale whose closing click was an email or organic visit is not credited to the ad that first brought the buyer in; that is why it reads lower than a tag-based count. MOF campaigns are kept out of every total above their own section. Revenue includes subscription rebills only when the buyer's last click was a Fall Summit ad and their lead was created on or after the first spend day; rebills from earlier leads are left out. Campaigns with no activity are left out. Ads and creatives are deliberately not shown.
